# Singapore itinerary planning

Visit Singapore publishes destination itineraries for different trip lengths and traveller interests. The collection includes short city itineraries as well as longer plans and themed guides.

## Two-day city exploration

The city-explorer itinerary is positioned around Singapore's cultural experiences, museums and major attractions. It is useful as a starting point when a traveller has limited time and wants a mix of culture and sightseeing.

## Four-day and longer itineraries

Visit Singapore also provides longer plans covering shopping, food, nature, urban attractions and themed experiences. The itinerary collection includes a four-day Singapore plan, a seven-day plan and themed guides such as food, wellness, honeymoon, shopping and outdoor adventures.

## Traveller-specific planning

The collection includes plans aimed at different traveller profiles, including solo travellers, families and groups. This makes the source useful for adapting a base itinerary to traveller preferences rather than treating one itinerary as suitable for everyone.
