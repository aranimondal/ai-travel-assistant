# Kampong Gelam

Kampong Gelam is presented by Visit Singapore as a neighbourhood where heritage and contemporary culture meet. The area around Kampong Gelam, Bugis and Bras Basah combines cultural landmarks, historic buildings, street art, shops and food.

## Culture and heritage

Sultan Mosque is highlighted as a major architectural and cultural landmark and an important focal point for Singapore's Muslim community. Hajjah Fatimah Mosque is another example of the area's cross-cultural architectural influences.

## Walking and street experiences

Haji Lane is described as a narrow neighbourhood street known for murals, independent businesses and boutique shops. Visit Singapore also highlights walking routes through Bugis, Waterloo Street and Kampong Gelam.

## Food

The neighbourhood combines traditional fare with contemporary dining. Examples highlighted by Visit Singapore include nasi lemak and other local dishes, alongside cafes and modern food businesses.

## Transport

Bugis MRT is identified as the nearest train station for the Kampong Gelam area.
